libusb is linked as the separate shared library libusb-1.0.so. Unmodified source
files, Android configuration, AUTHORS and per-file hashes are provided with this
project under android/native-usb/vendor/libusb and android/native-usb/sources.json.
The separate diagnostic JNI wrapper and CMake build recipe are in
android/native-usb. Rebuild instructions are in android/NATIVE_USB_TEST.md.

No libusb sample audio/firmware application is included or executed.
