Local Music - deferred until the stable version

User requested keeping these ideas for later, while addressing stability first.
Research compared popular Android music players on 5 November 2026.

1. Sorting by artist/album/year/duration/filename and disc-then-track album order.
2. Manage/remove chosen folders, exclude subfolders and repair lost access.
3. Drag reordering and bulk queue/playlist entry actions.
4. Genre browsing and better multi-artist/compilation organization.
5. Search filenames/folders/lyrics, separate words, typos and grouped results.
6. Listening history, play counts and rule-based smart playlists.
7. Artwork colors, library grid sizes and configurable Home sections.
8. Word-synced/TTML lyrics and line timing editor.
9. Parametric EQ, broader AutoEQ formats, speed/pitch and headphone presets.

Optional: custom playlist covers, saved queues, scrobbling and richer car browsing.
Favorites stays removed. No streaming expansion is scheduled.
High-resolution/bit-perfect and Direct USB remain separately deferred.
