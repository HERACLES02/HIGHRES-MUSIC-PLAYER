Subject: KZ M2 USB clock synchronization documentation (VID 31b2, PID 0311)

Draft only -- not sent.

We are investigating direct USB playback to a KZ M2 identified as KTMicro,
USB VID 31b2, PID 0311. Recorded descriptors show UAC1 playback interface 2,
stereo PCM16 (alternate 1) and packed PCM24 (alternate 2), with advertised
44.1, 48, 88.2 and 96 kHz rates. Native reports identify a full-speed link.

Playback endpoint 0x04 has attributes 0x05 (asynchronous isochronous OUT),
bInterval 1, bSynchAddress 0, and no accompanying explicit feedback endpoint.
There is a separate mono asynchronous capture endpoint 0x84 at 48 kHz;
we have not assumed it shares a playback clock or used it for timing.

Could you provide technical documentation answering:

1. What exact chip and firmware correspond to this VID/PID and descriptor layout?
2. How should the host synchronize playback to this firmware? Does the sink
   recover its audio clock from USB SOF/data flow, require an undocumented
   feedback/control path, or use another mechanism? Please explain the
   asynchronous descriptor and absent associated feedback endpoint.
3. Is there documented host-readable render-clock error, FIFO occupancy,
   underrun/overrun or dropped/repeated-sample telemetry? What are its units,
   timing and read semantics?
4. Does Standard mode bypass EQ, noise gating, compression, sample-rate
   conversion and other processing? What does equal left/right UAC1 Feature
   Unit 7 volume mean relative to internal per-channel gain/calibration?

Successful USB submissions and selected-rate readbacks are not being treated
as measurements of rendered samples or proof of clock synchronization.
